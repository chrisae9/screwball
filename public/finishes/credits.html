Tile artwork

The illustrations and patterns below were scaled, cropped, and recolored for the board.

Illustrations

  • Calico Quilt uses Sleepy Calico Cat by missiridia.
  • Midnight Mouser uses Black Cat by kattekrab.
  • Koi Pond uses Koi by stilg4r.
  • Lantern Moth uses Luna Moth by Borboleta.org.
  • Ashborn Phoenix uses Sunset Phoenix Emblem by Falco276.

These illustrations are from Openclipart’s public domain collection.

  • Vault 101 and Nuka-Cola Caps use Delapouite’s Fallout Shelter and Bottle Cap. Pip-Boy Terminal uses Lorc’s Radioactive. The symbols were recolored and framed for the tiles.
  • Dragon Scale, Mirror Fox, and Scarab Relic use Lorc’s Dragon Head, Fox Head, and Gold Scarab.
  • Neon Junction and Kraken Ink use Delapouite’s Railway and Kraken Tentacle.

These illustrations are from Game-icons.net under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0.

Volleyball uses the Fluent Emoji volleyball by Microsoft, scaled and shaded under its MIT license.

Fan designs

Vault 101, Pip-Boy Terminal, and Nuka-Cola Caps are unofficial tile designs inspired by Bethesda’s Fallout.

Kirby’s Warp Star is an original fan illustration inspired by Kirby from Nintendo and HAL Laboratory.

The unofficial Jett and Sage designs draw on the agents’ wind and jade abilities in VALORANT by Riot Games. The Tracer and D.Va designs draw on the chronal accelerator and mech pilot in Overwatch by Blizzard Entertainment.
